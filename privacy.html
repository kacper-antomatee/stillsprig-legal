2. Summary in plain language

  • The App plans a photo scene from things you describe: the kind, shape, size and colour of one product, the table surface, which side the window is on, and the props you own. You choose every value from a fixed list or type a measurement; there is no free-text field and the App never asks for photos.
  • The layouts, the setup diagram, the steps and the checklist are computed on your phone from our own catalog. Saved scenes ("My Looks") and exported PDF or JSON files stay on your phone.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you ask for an AI remix of a scene (StillSprig Pro). Then, and only then, the scene description above (not a photo, not your name) and the id of the layout you are looking at are sent to our server and passed to our AI provider. You see a consent screen before the first such request and can withdraw consent in Settings.
  • We keep the AI result on our server for 24 hours so that a retry of the same request does not cost you another use of your monthly allowance. Content you report is kept for 90 days.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos. We use a random anonymous identifier to apply usage limits and to attach a subscription to your installation, and a one-way hash of your device's Android ID so that reinstalling the App does not reset the monthly allowance.
  • You can delete all local and server data from the App's Settings (section 9).

3. Data we process, by category

3.1 Data that never leaves your device

  • Scene briefs: the product kind, outline, measurements and colours, the surface, the window side and the props you select, as closed-list values and numbers.
  • The results: the chosen layout, the diagram, steps and checklist, and the scenes you save under a name in "My Looks".
  • Files you export (a PDF setup card or a JSON scene file) go only to the app you pick in the system share sheet; they do not pass through our server. JSON files you import are checked against our scene format on the device and never uploaded.
  • Your settings: appearance, the weekly reminder (a local notification), the AI consent state.

Android may include the App's local data in the device backup to your Google Account (Android Backup). That copy is made and encrypted by Google under your Google Account settings; we have no access to it. You can exclude the App from backups in the Android settings.

7. Retention

Retention periods are stated per data type in section 3.2. Time-based deletion is applied by our database automatically; expired records are never served, and physical deletion completes within 72 hours of expiry. Accounting records of purchases are kept for the period required by Polish accounting and tax law (six full calendar years from the end of the year of the transaction), without the link to your anonymous identifier once you delete your data. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the App.

9. Your rights and how to exercise them

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzedu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

How to exercise them:

  • Withdraw AI consent: Settings -> "AI and privacy" -> "Withdraw consent". No further brief is sent until you allow it again.
  • Delete everything: Settings -> "Delete all my data". The App first asks our server to delete all records tied to your anonymous identifier (cached AI results, reports, usage counters, entitlement records) and the anonymous identity itself, then removes every saved scene and setting from the device. This cannot be undone. A subscription, if any, stays active in Google Play and can be restored on a new installation.
  • Without the App: write to sprzedaz@amazinggirl.pl. Because we hold no name or email, include the approximate date of first use and, if you bought StillSprig Pro, the Google Play order number, so that we can locate the record. Most server data also expires automatically (section 3.2). Instructions: https://kacper-antomatee.github.io/stillsprig-legal/delete-data.html.
  • Access, portability and other rights: write to sprzedaz@amazinggirl.pl. We answer within one month. Your saved scenes can be exported from the App at any time as JSON or PDF files (portability on the device, without our involvement).

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). The App contains no server credentials or AI provider keys. Our server verifies every request with a signed token and checks that it comes from a genuine copy of the App. Content fields are removed from logs. Access to production systems is limited to named team members. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. AI-generated content and automated processing

AI remixes (alternative layout picks and setup steps) are generated automatically by an AI model from the scene brief you provide. They are labelled "Generated by AI" in the App and on exported cards. The model can only choose among layouts that already passed our catalog rules and may only refer to props you listed; our server rejects steps that mention anything else, but it cannot judge whether the result will photograph well. You can report any AI result from the App. Reports are used to improve the quality and safety of generated content; they do not train the AI provider's models under our provider terms. We make no decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based on automated processing.

12. Notifications

The weekly reminder is created locally on your device, only when you have enabled it, and at most once a week. No data is sent to us to deliver it.
