2. What the App does

The App helps you arrange one small product and a few props on a table by a window for a photograph taken from above. You describe what you have by choosing from fixed lists and typing measurements; the App matches that description against our own catalog of layouts, draws a top-view diagram, and gives you the order of setup and a checklist. You can save scenes on your device ("My Looks") and export them as PDF or JSON files. On request, and only then, the App sends the description of your scene (never a photo) to our server, where an AI model picks alternative layouts from those that fit and writes a short setup guide (StillSprig Pro). Your saved scenes are stored on your device. There is no sync, backup or sharing between devices or users in the current version.

The diagram is a plan of positions, not a simulation of the photograph. The App makes no promise about light, colours or the final image.
